02 / THE FINE PRINT

What’s in the book

The player list combines current NZC central contracts with anyone recorded playing for New Zealand in a men's Test, ODI or T20I during the rolling 24 month window. A player can appear in both groups.

Performances come from Cricsheet’s open ball by ball archive. “All formats” means every match in that archive during the window, including domestic and franchise competitions. It does not mean every cricket match played worldwide. Some leagues, games and recent scorecards may be missing or arrive late. Super overs are excluded; batting balls exclude wides and bowling runs exclude byes and leg byes.

Each score opens its match scorecard on ESPNcricinfo. The match data and player identities are credited to Cricsheet; contract information comes from New Zealand Cricket. This is an independent project and is not affiliated with NZC.

    Where the numbers come from

    The list is everyone on a New Zealand Cricket contract, central or casual, plus anyone who's played a Test, ODI or T20 for New Zealand in the past twelve months.

    The scores come from Cricsheet, a free archive of ball-by-ball data. It covers internationals, first-class and List A cricket and most of the big T20 leagues, but not everything, and new games can take a few days to turn up.

    The biggest gap is New Zealand A. Cricsheet doesn't cover A-team cricket, so those games won't show up here. If someone looks quiet, they might just be off on an A tour.

    It updates itself every morning. Open up a player to see where each score came from. Each one links to its scorecard on ESPNcricinfo.

    A star means not out. Fifties and three-fors are in bold, hundreds and five-fors in red. Wides don't count as balls faced, and byes don't count against the bowler.
